# Research decision

Labels: Verified facts / Inference / Hypothesis / Unavailable evidence are explicit. This is a single-seed adapted-native comparison, not a paper reproduction or mechanism identification.

1. **Implementation fidelity:** qualified YES for inference comparison of these saved runs. Original model/loss/planner code is used with compatibility patches; common evaluations succeeded. NO to exact upstream training-loop reproduction: TD-MPC custom warmup/budget/schedule differ, and TD-MPC2 warmup Box seeding is incomplete in inspected source. The historical TD-MPC2 dirty harness is not fully source-hashed. This is not evidence that native TD-MPC2 objectives were replaced.
2. **Shared evaluation validity:** YES for the recorded contract, hashes, aggregate arithmetic, common physics/data cohort, validation-only selection and test-budget matching. NOT a bitwise-determinism or cross-wrapper CFD-equivalence certificate. TD-MPC best/final contain identical tensors yet yield slightly different scores; residual numerical/planner variability exists and should be measured. Train and val/test remain separate.
3. **Nominal TD-MPC2 competence:** some competence is verified: positive3.22% mean-drag reduction relative to the common uncontrolled TRAIN reference and legal 80-step trajectories. Negative reward does not mean no control benefit. Sufficiency is task-dependent; no required nominal threshold was specified. The48k degradation and the much weaker baseline than TD-MPC make a sole-primary robustness claim premature.
4. **Primary analysis algorithm:** retain TD-MPC2 as a candidate and native baseline; do not discard it based on one seed or promote it as a validated sole primary mechanism-study system. Retain TD-MPC as comparator. No method is generally superior on this evidence.
5. **Smallest high-information next experiment:** fixed44k checkpoint, two validation seeds, MPC vs actor mean:320 simulator steps with identical weights and measured latency. A result where actor substantially exceeds MPC would motivate model/value/planning diagnostics; similar poor outcomes would shift attention toward learned policy/model training competence. Neither proves a causal explanation. Then test48k and planner seed variation if needed; do not tune on test.
6. **Reusable diagnostics:** hashed checkpoint identity, fixed physical/source/data cohort, explicit split, separate environment/planner seed streams, common aggregation, same-action adapter checks, latency distributions and paired frozen-checkpoint ablations can be reused under a separately specified shift contract. Do not implement shift now.
